# Processing Machines

Processing machines convert materials. Most are powered machines with 1 input slot, 1 output slot, a battery slot, and 4 upgrade slots. Default EU cost is **32 E/t** and default processing time is **72 ticks** (3.6 seconds), unless stated otherwise.

## Cane Converter (Alchemy Block)

![growableores_block](../../img/industrial_elixir/machines/growableores_block_front.png)

A simple 1-input → 1-output converter used to turn **Ore Canes** into ingots. Tier 1 machine.

**Usage:** Place an ore cane in the input slot, power the machine, and collect the ingot.

## Iron Furnace

![iron_furnace](../../img/industrial_elixir/machines/iron_furnace_front.png)

An upgraded vanilla furnace. Burns solid fuel, uses the vanilla furnace GUI, and smelts with the standard smelting recipes.

## Electric Furnace

![electric_furnace](../../img/industrial_elixir/machines/electric_furnace_front.png)

An electric smelting furnace. Tier 1 machine. Smelts using vanilla smelting recipes but powered by energy instead of fuel.

## Induction Furnace

![induction_furnace](../../img/industrial_elixir/machines/induction_furnace_front.png)

A two-input smelting furnace with a heat mechanic. Tier 2 machine. Processes both input slots at once (up to 16 E/t per slot).

**Usage:** The furnace builds heat (0–100) while running. The hotter it is, the less energy is required per item — from **6000 E** per item down to **195 E** per item. A redstone signal keeps the heat from decaying (invert with a Redstone Inverter upgrade).
